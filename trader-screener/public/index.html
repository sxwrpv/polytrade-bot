Method

What each number is, and is not

  • Copy Score is a trader-level verdict on their finished record after copy costs, not a forecast and not a grade on any individual trade. It assumes every one of their trades was copied exactly; your filters, budget and timing will change it.
  • A missing metric is shown as unavailable, never as zero. A wallet with no volume in a window has no ROI — it is not a 0% wallet.
  • Leaderboard figures are live from Polymarket for the selected 7D, 30D or all-time window. Copy Score is a separate dated research overlay and is unavailable for wallets outside that scored cohort.
  • Angle boards are measured inside one slice only — resolved trades in that exact market and bet type, not overall profit. A wallet can lead a board and still be losing overall.
  • Past wallet activity does not predict future results.

Reading the board

  • Proven — copying them has worked on their finished trades.
  • Ahead — they finish ahead of what copying costs, but only just.
  • Caution — they make money; it does not survive being copied.
  • Losing — losing on their own trades, before copy costs start.
  • Not comparable — they close out early, so the number is not measuring the same thing.

Wallets flagged Market maker, Arbitrage or Very high frequency are harder to mirror: the edge lives in the spread, in holding both sides, or in speed a copy cannot match.
